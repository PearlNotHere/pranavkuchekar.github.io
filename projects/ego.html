Interaction & UI/UX · Game UI · Unity 6

EGO

A 2D action game with a single-element HUD: one eye shows health and mood in the iris, resource charge in the sclera and threat proximity in the pupil, so the player reads their whole state without leaving the action.

Combat rewards restraint: a 0.2s parry window favours timing over speed, and an overflow threshold penalises greed.
